# Pepper Seed Textures

Six replacement seed icons were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-10: Serrano, Cayenne, Poblano, Bell Pepper, Banana Pepper, and Ghost Pepper. The existing Jalapeno and Habanero icons were used as style references and remain unchanged.

Runtime files are `assets/peppermod/textures/item/seeds/{type}.png`, exported as 256x256 RGBA PNGs. The user approved image-processing cleanup and resizing. The first generation painted a checkerboard; subsequent generations used a magenta removal background. Both backgrounds were removed, edge fringes trimmed at source resolution, and icons scaled to match the existing inventory footprint. Original generated images remain in Codex's generated-image storage, and replaced runtime files are backed up locally in the Git-ignored `backups/seed-textures-before-2026-09-10` folder.

This is artwork only. Seed item IDs, planting behavior, and wild-spawning eligibility are unchanged. The finished comparison sheet is `art/previews/pepper-seeds.png`.

## Prompt Set

### serrano

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single transparent inventory seed icon for a Vintage Story pepper mod. Create a new variety-specific variation of the attached seed artwork. The attached Jalapeno and Habanero icons are STYLE REFERENCES; do not change their source files or reproduce their exact composition. Match their rustic hand-painted voxel-game inventory-art language: softly blocky crisp edges, restrained pixel clusters, flat faceted pale seeds, warm ochre creases, gentle upper-left highlights and brown shaded edges. Moderate painterly detail, not glossy 3D, not photorealistic, not a deliberately ultra-low-resolution mosaic. Keep all seeds naturally pale ivory/gold, rather than making seeds the pepper color. Each seed should read as a thin flattened disk, not a bean or corn kernel. A small pepper fragment identifies the variety, but seeds occupy at least 70 percent of the object's footprint. Compact centered three-quarter overhead composition, filling about 75 percent of the square, all parts inside the frame with transparent padding. No ground plane, no cast shadow beyond the object, no text, no labels, no border, no badge, no watermark. Deliver ONE standalone square PNG icon, ideally 1024x1024, with a genuinely transparent alpha background, including gaps between seeds. The transparent background must NOT be painted black, white, pink, or checkerboard. Serrano: a loose, low crescent-shaped scatter of about ten pale ivory-gold flat oval seeds, some face-up and some tilted, with one small slender deep-green cut pepper end lying diagonally behind the UPPER LEFT of the seeds. The green piece must be noticeably longer and narrower than a jalapeno stub. Distinct from both reference compositions.
